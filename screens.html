Start and end screens

The real app components, each seeded into its own frame (390 × 900). Nothing here touches your save. Death, the choice and the revive toast replay by themselves; reload a frame to replay the others.

Starting

First launch, in order: name the hero, the figure's opening, then the empty board. After a prestige: the pick wheel, then the figure's return line.

Dying

The fall opens straight on Revive OR Rise again (with and without gems for a revive). "Rise again" continues the same run with the setback; there is no separate new-run screen after a death.

Retiring

Prestige popup at three ranks (it changes with the rank), then the high-score sheet the run lands on.
