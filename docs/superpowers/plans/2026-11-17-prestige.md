# Prestige Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (the user runs plans inline). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Death becomes a setback instead of a reset. Prestige becomes the player-chosen reset, a five-rank ladder (I to V) that pays stats, gems and one special per rank. It shows as the Crown plinth on the Talents tab and the statue popup.

**Architecture:** The rank stays on `Account.prestige`, and that is the source of truth. A copy goes on `Character.prestige` so the pure stat math (`itemBonusPercent`, talent max ranks) can read it without new parameters at every call site. The boss gets a shown number (`Boss.shownIndex`) next to its power `index`. Every stat and price keeps reading `index`, and every label reads `bossNumber(boss)`. The old death reset moves almost word for word into `prestigeRun`, and `resolvePlayerDeathIfDead` becomes the setback.

**Tech Stack:** Vue 3, Pinia, TypeScript, Vitest, SCSS (Pixel-cut look in `src/assets/scss/themes/ashen.scss`), lucide-vue-next.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-prestige-design.md`. Mocks: `docs/mockups/prestige-tree.html` (option **A · Crown plinth**) and `docs/mockups/prestige-popup.html` (option **A · The statue**).

## Global Constraints

- Ranks: I Veteran L30, II Veteran L40, III Ascendant L50, IV Ascendant L60, V Mythic L70. The bar reads the **current** level.
- Each rank gives +20% `allDamage`, +10% `health` and +5% `expGain`, added into the same sum as items and talents (no separate multiplier), plus 50 gems paid at prestige. Add `FREE_GEM_SOURCES.prestige: 250`.
- Specials: I free dungeon, II Deeper roots, III free dungeon, IV Deeper roots II, V Mythic skin (one per class, placeholder art).
- Death: −3 levels (never below 1), −25% of carried gold, −1 random equipped item, next boss at power P−3 (never below 1) shown as the same number. No perk softens it, and nothing protects a streak.
- No hidden boss buffs. Bosses are never touched by rank.
- The engine stays pure. All saving goes through the stores. Old saves with no `prestige`, `deaths`, `shownIndex` or `freeDungeons` read as 0, absent, or the same as `index`.
- Phone first: tap targets of at least 44px, safe areas respected, nothing behind hover. Animations use transform and opacity only, and are dropped under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Copy is terse and a little mysterious. Item and rank effects are stated plainly. The look is Pixel-cut (Moonlit), with the rare, epic and relic tier colours from `PrestigeNumeral.vue`.
- Revive: always open on the death screen while the account holds `REVIVE_GEMS` (10). There is no once-per-boss limit (the user, 2026-11-17).
- Don't push. Commit locally on `main` only.

## Review Focus

- **Reviving twice against one boss.** A revive is always open while the gems cover it: a second and a third fall against the same boss each offer Revive again, and an old save's `revivedAtBoss` changes nothing (test in Task 4).
- **Dying at level 1–3, with no gold or no items.** The level floors at 1, nothing turns NaN, and the bonfire shows only the losses that apply (tests in Tasks 4 and 7).
- **An old save mid-run.** A save with a boss that has no `shownIndex`, a character with no `prestige`, or an account with `prestige: 2` (the dev button) but a character with none loads with the stats synced (tests in Tasks 1 and 3).
- **Double-tapping Prestige, or prestiging while dead or on the new-hero screen.** The second call is a no-op, and `canPrestige` is false while dead or while `heroPending` is set (tests in Tasks 1 and 6).
- **A top node after Deeper roots.** Mains at their *base* max still open the ★, and a ★ never gains a rank (test in Task 2).
