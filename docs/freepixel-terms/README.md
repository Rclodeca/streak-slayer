- `*.html` is the raw page as served (`curl`). `*.txt` is the page text with the
  site navigation stripped; nothing in the terms themselves was changed.
- The Terms of Service say "Last updated: September 19, 2026". The source art
  was downloaded between February and March 2026 (see the dates in the
  filenames in CREDITS.md), so these are not necessarily the exact terms in
  force at download time. The license page carries no revision date.
- Points to remember: commercial use and app stores are allowed, no
  attribution is required, and raw asset files may not be redistributed (only
  the processed sprites live in the repo). ToS section 5 says assets are made
  "using a combination of tools and techniques, including generative
  technology", with no warranty. The terms state no duration or revocation
  terms.
