Potion or service bought — no modal

A small note that says what the potion did and goes by itself, so you can keep shopping. Tap any potion's price, or Replay. The Health Potion also fills the HP bar in the corner as it lands.

A · Toast above the tabs (recommended)

A short card slides up over the bottom of the list: the potion, its name, what it did. A line drains along its foot and it fades after about 3 seconds. Easy to read, out of the way of the rows.

B · Floating number

The potion and its effect float up off the row's button and fade, like a hit number in a fight. The most game-like and the least in the way, but gone fast and easy to miss.

C · Ribbon under the scene

A thin strip slides down from under the shopkeeper's scene, tinted by the potion. Reads like the shop answering you, but it covers the top of the list for a moment.

D · Item slots, same toast

Buy a slot and the card stays put; the toast says "5 → 6 slots" and fades. Buy again right away and it just updates, no tap needed to clear anything.

E · Overdrive, same toast

Pick the skill in the picker (here a stand-in list) and confirm: the picker closes and the toast names the skill. Reroll and Enchant keep their popups, since their result is the news.
