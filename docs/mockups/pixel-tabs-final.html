Pixel-cut · final tabs

The bottom bar: Skills · Quests · Trials · Hero. Every phone is live: tap the bar, tap the hero, open the menu, fold Resist, strike skills, check off quests.

What's in it
  • Skills keeps Daily, Resting and Weekly, then Resist at the foot. Resist folds shut, with its count and its own plus. It starts open.
  • Quests are only the ones you write. No Main / Side switch. Badge only when one is due today or overdue.
  • Trials are the old side quests. No add button. A pip when a new one arrives, gone once you open the tab.
  • Hero replaces the hero popup. Tapping the hero on the stage opens it too. It starts at level and best streak, then effects, gear and stats. The boss stays a popup.
  • Trials and Hero use the 76px Focus strip, since you read those pages and don't strike from them. Skills and Quests keep the full stage. The switch animates.
  • Log is in the menu and opens as a sheet. It replaces the old "Show log" toggle.
  • New players (level under 3): Quests and Trials are greyed with a lock but still open, showing the seal. They unlock together at level 3.
  • Light: grey page, white cards, gunmetal Resist and a white bar. The stage stays night, and the Log sheet takes Dusk like the other popups.
