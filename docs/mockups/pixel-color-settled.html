Colour themes · damage colours as the accents

Each theme picks only the quiet colours (page, cards, nav bar, outline). Things you touch (the main button, badges) are a pale tint of the theme's own hue, the selected tab is a lighter card, and levels and EXP are a soft, muted gold. The four damage colours are the only bright colours on screen.

How it works
  • Bases are blue, teal, warm stone and slate, kept away from red (physical), orchid (magic) and green (healing).
  • Damage hues never change. Each theme tunes their lightness and saturation so they sit in its light, and nudges a hue a few degrees where the base leans close.
  • Tuned damage colours off shows the game's current four on every theme, to compare.
