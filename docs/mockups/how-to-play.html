HOW TO PLAY

Where it lives in the menu, then four ways it could look. Each one is clickable: page through A, open entries in B, step through C.

Menu · plain

"How to play" sits right after High Scores, with the open book. Always there, no fuss.

Menu · lit for new heroes

Same row, lit brass with a pip until it's been opened once (or until level 5). The cog wears the pip too, so a new player finds it.

A · Pages

A short picture book: six pages, one idea each, one big icon and two lines. Swipe or Next. Fastest to read, easy to re-open on any page.

B · Codex

A list of topics, tap to open. Things you haven't unlocked yet stay sealed with their level, so it doesn't spoil what's coming. Grows with you.

C · Walkthrough

No new screen: it replays spotlight hints over the real game, lighting each part in turn (stage, a habit, Resist, tabs). Teaches where things are, not just what.

D · The Oath

One screen, six lines, like the start screen's voice. Cheapest to build and to keep up to date. No paging, no depth.

  • My pick: B (Codex) with the lit menu row. It's the only one that keeps the mystery (sealed entries) while still answering "what does this do" later on, when Shop, Talents and prestige show up.
  • Mix: A's pages could be the first time it opens, then it falls back to B every time after.
  • Copy: placeholder, kept in the terse game voice. Numbers only where a new player needs them (death costs).
