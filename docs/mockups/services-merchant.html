Services — making them worth a look

Item slots, Streak Freeze and Gold Pouch stay on the shelf in all three: people plan around them. A is the one change I'd make no matter what, and it's in B and C too. B and C are two ways to offer the fun services only some of the time. Numbers are stand-ins.

A · On your stuff

Every row shows what it would do to your own best target, before and after. A new player gets "Steel Sword +5% → +10%," not "×2 one worn item's bonuses." All six stay on the shelf every day.

B · The Wanderer (recommended)

After a boss falls, a wanderer may come to the shop with two services, cheaper, for a day. The Shop tab gets a pip. It ties services to winning fights, and the visit is an event, not a list.

C · Service of the day

One of the fun services is on the velvet each day, the way the featured relic is, and the strip shows the week's turn. Easy to read, but it needs a bigger pool than three to feel like a turn.
