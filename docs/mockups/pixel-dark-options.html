What I weighed
  • Not black. Today's page is about 14% light, so the pixel bevels have nowhere darker to go: the lips and shadows that give the cards their shape vanish into the page. These sit around 21–23%, so a shadow can still be darker than what it falls on.
  • Depth climbs in steps. Page, stage, panel, card, open card each a little lighter than the one below, as light would fall. Struck cards sink back down.
  • The art needs a lighter ground. The boss and item sprites carry a dark outline. On near-black it disappears; on a lifted ground it reads as a drawn edge again.
  • Tinted, not grey. A touch of one hue gives the stone a mood. Text and silver share that hue, and nothing is pure white (ink tops out near 97%).
  • Text holds 7:1. Lifting the ground costs contrast, so body text is lighter than today's. The numbers under each phone are live.
  • Accents left alone this round (damage colours, brass, EXP gold, rarity), so you judge the stone only. They may want a small nudge afterwards.
