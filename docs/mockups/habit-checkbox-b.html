Habits · Checkbox B

The real app with sample habits. In B, tap a box to check a habit off (it runs the real strike), and tap the card for its details as usual. Undo is in the menu, like today.

  • Box: pixel box at the card's left edge, rimmed in the habit's damage colour, 44px tap area.
  • Checked: it flashes the damage colour, then settles grey with the card, like a struck key does today.
  • Damage: moves into the PWR line under the name.
  • Bad habits: a red-rimmed box. Checking it means you gave in, and it fills with ✕. The line under the name reads "If resisted" and the damage.
