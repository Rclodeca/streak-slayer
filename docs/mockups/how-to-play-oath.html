HOW TO PLAY · THE OATH

Menu: the plain row after High Scores. Five ways to word the popup. Same six ideas each time, no death line. Each is written so the game and real life line up.

  • My pick: 1 · Plain. Every line says what you do and what happens, in everyday words, and the last line ties it all to real life. 3 · Story is close behind if you want more flavour.
  • Easy swaps: any option's last line works in any other, and so does the eyebrow.
  • Accurate to the game: a bad habit you avoid all day strikes the boss when the day turns; giving in makes the boss strike you. Each day of a streak adds to a habit's hits, and a miss starts the streak over. A task done by its due date pays EXP; a late one hurts.
