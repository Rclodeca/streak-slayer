Shop — Services art

Same six services in the real order, three ways to show them. Each phone starts with two rows from the Items side so you can see the contrast. Glyphs are quick hand-drawn stand-ins; real ones would get a proper pass.

A · Today

The line icons from the app, in the same square socket that items use. Clean and clearly "UI", but they sit in an item socket and don't feel like the game's art.

B · Seals (recommended)

Pixel art, but on a round medallion with no socket and no rarity. Brass for gold buys, frost silver for gem buys. The round shape alone says "not an item," even on the backpack and coin bag. Locked: the seal goes to dull iron with a padlock on its rim, and the button shows the level it opens at.

C · Item-style art (the risk)

The same pixel art in full colour, in the square socket. The backpack and coin bag now look like loot you'd wear, and there's no inventory to find them in.
