TASKS · REDESIGN

Same 30px box in a 44px tap as a habit, same card cut and 58px height. Difficulty dots are gone. Tasks get their own identity from brass (the Tasks colour) and how each shows the due date. Tap a box to check it off, tap a group header, chip or folder to try grouping.

    Options

    A · Ledger (my pick)

    Brass box. Due sits in a small tab on the card's corner, like a habit's streak tab. Groups are headers in the list; tap one to fold it.

    B · Coloured groups

    The box rim takes the group's colour, the way a habit's box takes its damage type. Chips at the top filter by group. Due words under the name.

    C · Notice board

    Tasks are notes on a warm board, not slate cards: the most different from habits. Groups are folder tabs. Due is a stamp on the right.

    D · Calendar stub

    A tear-off date on the right end (day number, month) lit by urgency, so the due date is the task's signature. Group headers like A.

    E · Stub + coloured groups

    D's calendar stub on the right, B's group colour on the box and group chips at the top. The group's icon and name sit under the task name with the due words.

    Groups · preset or your own

    What the New task popup would show. Every option above works with any of these.

    P1 · Six presets

    Fixed names and icons. Simplest. Some players won't fit (no "Kids", "Side project").

    P2 · Six presets, renamable (my pick)

    Same six slots and icons; long-press or Edit to rename one. No create or delete, so almost no extra complexity.

    Rename

    P3 · Make your own

    Any number of groups, name + icon + colour. Needs create, edit, delete, reorder, and what happens to tasks in a deleted group.

    • No group: a task can stay ungrouped; ungrouped tasks sit at the top with no header. With one group in use, headers hide entirely.
    • Order: groups in a fixed order, soonest due first inside each, checked-off tasks stay in place until the day turns (as today).
    • Overdue burns red in each option; done drops grey and flat like a checked habit.
