Shop tab

The Shop takes the Trials tab's place. The shopkeeper backdrop is pinned at the top like the armory, with your gold and gems on the art. The wares are stand-ins from the shop idea doc: gold buys things for this run, gems buy things you keep. Everything is tappable.

A · On the counter

Tap a ware and the shopkeeper sets it on his counter, with a paper price tag and a line from him. The card under the art shows what it does and the Buy button. Wares sit below in two sockets grids: gold for this run, gems kept for good.

B · The shopkeeper's pick

He speaks first, then one rare of the run is shown off on velvet (flip through the three with ‹ ›). Under it, wares in rows like Skills, each with its price on the button, so it reads and buys like the rest of the game.

C · Two purses

A Gold | Gems switch splits the shop in two. Gold: item cards three across, then tonics with cooldowns and services whose price climbs (the pips). Gems: the slot track (6 of 8, the next one blinking), talent points, themes, and the rest.

D · Shelves

The tab is a wooden cabinet: items and tonic jars stand on shelves, each with a paper tag under it. Gem goods are behind glass in a cold vault cabinet. Tap anything and a sheet slides up with its card and Buy.
