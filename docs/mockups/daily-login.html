DAILY LOGIN GOLD

Three options. Each is shown on a day with habits to recap and on a day with nothing to recap. The gold is a placeholder: half a boss kill's gold at the current boss (here boss 6, 45 gold).

A · Merchant's purse

Its own popup, after the Daily update. The shop scene on top, one big gold number, Claim. On a day a new service comes up, the service rides along as a card that opens Shop → Services. With nothing to recap, the Daily update is skipped and only this shows.

B · Inside the Daily update

One popup. The gold sits on a lifted card at the top of the Daily update, the recap below it. The button pays both: "Claim" on a good day, "Take the blow" when the boss strikes. With nothing to recap, a quiet line takes the list's place, so the popup still shows every day.

C · Week of coins

Seven coin days, Monday to Sunday, Sunday pays gems. Today is lit. A missed day just stays dark: nothing resets, the week starts fresh each Monday. Shown inside the Daily update here, but it works as its own popup too (A's place).

  • Gold is paid on the button, so it lands in the stage purse as the popup closes.
  • Once a day, the first open of the day, the same moment the Daily update is decided. The day boundary is the habit day, not midnight.
  • C's Sunday gems (2 here) would come out of the free gem budget in economy.ts.
