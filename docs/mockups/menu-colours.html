Menu buttons: colours

All from the game's own palette (Midnight / Moonlit tokens), on a night scene and a bright one. The chip shape and size are the same as the black one.

Bare gold icons (#d8b46a, the EXP and reward gold) with a dark indigo rim instead of black.
