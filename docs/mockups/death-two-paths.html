Death: two paths

Ryan, level 14 warrior, fell at boss 6 carrying 200 gold and wearing 4 items. Every option: Revive (15 gems, half health, nothing lost) OR Rise again (full health, a weaker boss), and Rise again spells out its three losses: 1 level (14 → 13), 20 gold (200 → 180) and 1 item dropped.
