Death + after prestige

Hero Ryan, level 14 warrior, fell to Iron Guardian at boss 6, carrying 200 gold, revive 15 gems. The real numbers: a death takes 1 level (and that level's EXP), 10% of carried gold and one worn item at random, then the next boss is weaker (boss 6 at the strength of boss 3), and you rise at full health. A revive costs 15 gems and keeps everything, at half health.

Death

Hooded cutscene ("You died" + his line, as now) → setback page (below). C folds the cutscene into the stage instead.

A · The toll

One plain list of what rising costs, the weaker boss as the one good line, and two buttons. Short of gems, Revive greys and Get gems sits beside it.

B · Two paths

The choice is the page: a Revive card and a Rise card, each saying what it gives and what it costs. Short of gems, the Revive card shows how far off you are and Get gems takes its button.

C · On the stage

No separate cutscene: the fight stage itself darkens, the boss stands over you, his line runs under "You fell", and a sheet rises from the bottom with the toll and both buttons. It never leaves the game, but it drops the hooded figure from death.

After prestige

Prestige popup (the statue, as now) → hero pick (below) → hooded return line. Shown at Prestige I (rare blue); the page takes the new rank's colour, so III is violet and V ember.

A · Rank first

The new rank is the headline, the retirement one plain line, then the pick. Rules dropped: a veteran knows them.

B · The statue stays

Picks up straight from the popup: the retired hero stands in stone on the left; the new hero is picked by the fire beside it. One title, one line.

C · The ledger

Says plainly what changed: the rank's gains, what carries over and what starts again, then the pick. Most information, least ceremony.
