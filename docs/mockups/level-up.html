Three options for the level-up popup. Each one is shown at a level with no unlocks, one unlock and two. Change the unlock levels below to try other mixes, or use the ‹ › arrows to step any column's level.

Unlock levels (placeholders)

Shop opens with Items only. Services and Looks open inside it later. In code these would be one table of levels, not hard-coded.

A · Brass plate

The level in a brass diamond, since a level is earned. Stats are a ledger in a sunk well. Each unlock is a lifted card under an "Unlocked" rule. With no unlocks, the popup simply ends at the stats.

B · Night strip

The hero stands on the arena at the top of the popup with the new level beside them. Stats are five small tiles. One unlock is a wide card; two sit side by side as square tiles.

C · Unlocks lead

When something opens, its emblem is the headline (the Quests and Talents popups' silver plate), one or two side by side. The level and stats shrink to one strip below. With nothing new, the brass level takes the emblem's place.

  • Stats: health and healing +9.5% a level, physical, magic and true +6%. Numbers here are the base stats grown to the level, no items.
  • The heal line is the level-up heal (10% of max health).
  • These would replace the separate Quests and Talents unlock popups, so one level gives one popup.
