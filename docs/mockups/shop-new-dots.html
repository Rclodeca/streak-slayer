Shop "new" dots

A red dot on the Shop tab when the day has turned (new items) or a service has rotated, and on the Items or Services switch for whichever side is new. Opening a side clears it.

Try it

Tap the Shop tab, then Services, then Looks. Then turn the day.

  • The day turns: Items is new, and Shop wears a dot.
  • Every 3rd day the service rotates too: Services is new as well.
  • Open a side and its dot clears. The Shop dot stays while the other side is still unseen.
  • A new player also sees a dot on Looks, once. It clears when Looks is opened and never comes back.
  • The Skills, Quests and Talents badges are unchanged.
