Skills tab, other ideas

Four takes on the same 75px skill card, each solving something different. All use the real Moonlit tokens and today's Give in key. Tap keys to strike; tap Daily / Weekly in idea 1.

1 · Daily | Weekly switch

One list at a time. A two-key switch under the banner with a count on each, so the page stays short and the weekly skills stop hiding at the bottom.

2 · Rank progress

The taller card has room for a bar to the next rank, with the rank numeral beside it, in place of the row of pips. Streak stays at the end of the line.

3 · Colour spine

A 6px spine in the damage type's colour down the left edge, and the type's name under the skill. You can tell Physical from Magic before reading the key.

4 · Streak plate

The streak gets a sunk plate on the left, big and countable, like the Resist reward plate. The line under the name keeps the rank pips.
